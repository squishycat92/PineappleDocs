# Privacy Policy
The following privacy policy applies to both the bots Pineapple and Pineapple Testing, hereby referred to as "Pineapple".

## General
Pineapple never logs or shares your user data with third parties. By using Pineapple, you agree to the usage of your data as outlined in this document.

### Analytics and Errors
Analytics about command usage may be anonymously collected; they only include the frequency of use of the command to help focus development efforts. Errors are automatically reported and only include the command that triggered the error.

### Privileged Intents: Message Content
Pineapple uses message content for a variety of reasons. They include:
1. Message-based commands using the bot prefix (non slash commands).
2. Some auto-reaction roles and moderation features in Elbees Hive.
3. Internal links to the florr.io #game channel API.

Users' messages in Elbees Hive may be processed by the bot for automoderation and autoreaction features. Users' messages outside of Elbees Hive that do not start with the command prefix are never processed. Messages and message content are never logged or saved. Message content is only used when triggered by a command to pass arguments to bot features.

### Privileged Intents: Guild Members
Pineapple uses guild member data for a variety of reasons. They include:
1. Some role management commands, which use a member cache to avoid fetching API data when assigning many roles.
2. Automatic role assigning and access evaluation in Elbees Hive depending on other member data such as their role in the server or their florr.io inventory. Commands that assign a large number of roles at a time require the member cache to complete in a timely manner.

### Privileged Intents: Presence Updates
Pineapple uses presence (status) update data for a variety of reasons. They include:
1. Reporting the platforms that a user is online in the user information command.

To protect user privacy, Pineapple only reports presence information relating to a user's Discord status (Online, Idle, Do Not Disturb). Game and music activity is not reported and text-based (custom) statuses are not processed. User presence patterns and historical data is never processed by the bot. User presences are never logged.

## florr.io
Pineapple offers a link command to attach your florr.io account data to your user account. By using the link command, you agree that Pineapple will log your inventory, mob gallery, achievements, and other information pertaining to your in-game profile. Your in-game profile will be made publicly accessible for discovery by other players. When linking your profile, your account details will be retained by the bot in a database.

Pineapple never processes, logs, or retains sensitive account information such as your Support PIN.

Florr.io account data is also governed by the game [Terms of Service](https://florr.io/tos.txt) and [Privacy Policy](https://florr.io/pp.txt).
